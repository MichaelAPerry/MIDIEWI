A KUYIN digital piano listing also mentions a Kalezo app. Could you help us verify whether either product uses your technology?

- Did KALEZO supply the DC02's controller firmware, synthesis engine, sound bank or board? If this varies by hardware revision, what identifier should we check?

- Is there a documented, read-only way to identify the DC02 hardware or firmware version over USB MIDI or Bluetooth? If a service utility or recovery guide exists for this revision, could you point us to its official documentation?

Our priority is preserving the instrument's original firmware and documenting supported interoperability. Public documentation or a model/revision confirmation would be welcome. Please let us know if we may cite your reply.

Thank you for your time. [MIDIEWI](https://github.com/MichaelAPerry/MIDIEWI)
