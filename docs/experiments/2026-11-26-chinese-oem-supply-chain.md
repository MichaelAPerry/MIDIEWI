# 2026-11-26 Chinese OEM electronics supply-chain investigation

The USB manufacturer string in the original-style DC02 now has a credible corporate lead. A Chinese class-15 trademark listing links **KALEZO** to **深圳市凯乐奏电子有限公司**, and an electronic-instrument patent independently establishes the firm's work in the sector. Supply-chain searches also surfaced two photographed wind mainboards and a documented Dream-based synthesis module. None establishes which company supplied Konix or which processor runs the tested unit.

- A May 2023 Elecfans seller post markets complete wind and drum development, a standalone sound core, chips including SAM5504B and SAM5704B, and original development packages including 5000-SDK, 2695-EK and 5704FX-DK. The four image originals were retrieved. They show a labeled DREAM 5000-SDK B enclosure, a labeled 5704FX-DK and a compact USB-C sound-core board with a much higher lead-count IC than the photographed U6. The fourth image shows a closed wind instrument. The core photo has no defensible chip ID or revision match. The seller offers SDK and demos commercially without a public download attached.
