## Patent, copyright and public contact

The Chinese applicant name matches the KALEZO trademark index. Figure 1 and the claims describe recognizing speech as a command, then gating it by instrument state. The cited `CN113077769A` concerns music control and has a different applicant. The background also discusses `CN111785270A`, another electronic-keyboard control disclosure. Exact-name searches found no additional Kalezo-assigned synthesis or update patent in this pass. ## Shared platform assessment

Cross-category development and shared sound-production expertise are plausible and supported by the recruitment and employer evidence. The USB string and piano seller's app reference make a shared provider a credible hypothesis. They do not establish a shared embedded synth, chipset, patch format or firmware family. A common app could communicate over standard BLE MIDI across unrelated products. Stronger evidence would be OEM documentation naming the platform, or verified packages for distinct products containing a common engine and revision-specific identifiers.

The established non-destructive route for the DC02 remains ordinary USB MIDI and BLE-MIDI discovery. This pass found no additional documented DC02 query, maintenance service or app-based route. There was no application or firmware execution and no instrument change. [Draft bilingual request for confirmation](../development/kalezo-inquiry.md)
